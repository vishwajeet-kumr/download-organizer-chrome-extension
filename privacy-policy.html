Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. What We Collect

  • Download filenames: Processed entirely locally inside your browser to determine proper categorization; never transmitted to our servers.
  • Folder preferences: Stored securely in chrome.storage.sync across your connected Google Chrome profiles.
  • License key: Transmitted to our validation endpoint exclusively during manual activation and automated 7-day re-validation.

2. What We Do NOT Collect

  • No browsing history or web activity.
  • No visited URLs or website contents.
  • No file contents of any downloaded items.
  • No personal identification information, tracking tokens, or behavioral telemetry.

3. Third Parties

  • Dodo Payments (dodopayments.com): Merchant of record handling secure payment processing and license key generation.
  • Google Chrome Sync: Facilitates synchronization of user-configured folder preferences across authorized Chrome browser sessions.

4. Data Retention

Your license key and customized preferences remain saved locally inside your browser storage. We maintain zero personal user databases or tracking logs on our servers.

5. Contact

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this privacy policy, please reach out via our support email: support@provibal.com.
